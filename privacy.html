Bite

Privacy Policy

In effect from 3 October 2026

Bite is an app for keeping track of the places you eat and drink, and sharing them with friends. This policy says what Bite collects, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read.

What we collect

Your account: your email address, the name and username you choose, and your password. Passwords are stored scrambled; nobody at Bite can read them. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and name from them and never see your password.

What you tell us when you join: why you came, your gender, your age band, who you usually eat with and how often you eat out. Every one of these is optional and you can change or clear them in Settings.

What you put on Bite: the places you log, your scores, prices, descriptions, photos and videos, your Vault, guides, polls, comments, likes, and who you follow.

How you use the app: which screens you open and which actions you take, tied to your account. We use this to see what works and what is broken.

When something goes wrong in the app, we record what failed: the error, the screen you were on, your device model, its operating system version and the app version. It is tied to your account so we can tell how many people a fault affects. We use it only to fix bugs.

Your location, only while you use the map and only if you allow it. It is used on your phone to show where you are. We do not store it.

Photos you scan with the camera to find a place are sent to Google to read the text on them. They are not kept by Bite unless you attach them to a post.

Your contacts, only if you tap Find friends in your contacts and allow it. Bite reads the email addresses in your address book, scrambles each one on your phone so it cannot be read back, and sends only the scrambled versions to check against Bite accounts. Your contacts are never uploaded or stored, and you can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in your phone settings.

What we use it for

To run the app: showing your visits and guides, showing your friends' posts to you and yours to them, and sending the notifications you would expect.

To improve the app: counting what is used and finding faults.

To email you about your account and, unless you switch it off in Settings, about new features.

To keep people safe: acting on reports, removing content that breaks the rules, and removing accounts that abuse others.

To suggest people you may know: people followed by those you follow, people who have been to the same places as you, and, if you allowed it, contacts who are on Bite.

What other people can see

Your name, username and profile photo can be seen by anyone signed in to Bite.

If your account is public, your visits, scores, photos, guides and polls can be seen by anyone signed in. If you make your account private in Settings, only followers you have approved can see them.

Your Vault can be seen by you and by your friends. Friends are people you follow who also follow you.

A guide can be set to public, friends only, or private.

Your email address and the answers you gave when you joined are never shown to other users.

Someone you block cannot see your posts, follow you or comment, and you do not see theirs.

Who else handles your information

Supabase stores the database, the sign-in system and your photos and videos, on servers in Australia.

Google provides restaurant search, place details and photos, the map on Android, and the text reading used when you scan a storefront. The search terms and place you look up are sent to Google.

Apple provides the map on iPhone, and sign-in if you choose Sign in with Apple.

Resend delivers our emails.

Sentry receives a report when the app crashes or fails: the error, the screen, your device model and app version, and your account id. Never what you typed.

PostHog receives which screens you open and which actions you take, tied to your account id, and may record how the screen looked during a session with anything you type hidden. We use it to see where people get stuck.

Expo provides the tools the app is built with.

These companies handle information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your information. We do not show adverts and we do not share your information with advertisers.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, photos, videos, guides, comments, likes and follows are deleted straight away. Copies held in routine backups expire on their own shortly after.

Reports you made about other people are kept without your name attached, so that a pattern of abuse is not lost.

Your choices

Delete your account and everything in it: Settings, Delete account. It takes effect at once and cannot be undone.

Make your account private: Settings, Private account.

Change or clear the answers you gave when you joined: Settings, Your answers.

Stop feature emails: Settings, Product emails.

Block someone or report something: the three dots on any post, comment, guide or profile.

Turn off camera, microphone, photos or location access at any time in your phone's settings.

Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by writing to the address below. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Bite is not for anyone under 13. If you believe a child has made an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Where this applies

Bite is run from Singapore and follows Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act. If you use Bite from somewhere else, your information is still handled as this policy describes.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
