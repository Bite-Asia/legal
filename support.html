Support

Help with Bite. If your question is not answered here, write to us and a person will read it.

Signing in

Forgot your password: on the log in page, tap Forgot password. We email you a code to type into the app. If it does not arrive, check your junk or spam folder.

If you joined with Google or Apple, sign in with the same button again. There is no separate Bite password for those accounts.

Your account

Change your name or username in Settings. A username can be changed once, and not in the first 48 hours after joining.

Make your account private in Settings, Private account. Only followers you approve will see your posts.

Delete your account in Settings, Delete account. It takes effect at once, removes everything you posted, and cannot be undone.

Reporting and blocking

Tap the three dots on any post, comment, guide or profile to report it or to block the person. We review every report within 24 hours.

A place is wrong or missing

Place names, addresses and photos come from Google, so a place that is missing from Google will be missing from Bite. Try searching for it the way Google Maps spells it.

If the cuisine or the type of place is wrong, open the place in Bite and fix the tags. Your fix applies for everyone.

Your information

To ask for a copy of your information, or to have it corrected, write to the address below. We answer within 30 days. The Privacy Policy explains what Bite keeps and why.
